What the adapter changed

General speech models transcribe Awadhi by translating it into standard Hindi, and on longer clips they stop transcribing altogether and repeat a syllable until they run out of room. Both models below were decoded by the same script with the same settings on the same held-out utterances. The only difference is a 8.7 MB adapter trained for 52 minutes on one laptop GPU.

Quoted speech is the corpus's own human transcription, not model output. Narration is generated. Nothing in a quotation has been corrected, standardised or rewritten — a validator rejects the text if it has.
