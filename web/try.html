Your own voice

The model runs inside this page. Your audio is never uploaded, which is the point: a tool for keeping family recordings should not require handing them to someone else's server. The first run downloads about 250 MB and your browser keeps it cached.

Awadhi or Hindi speech works best. The model learned from 8 kHz recordings of people describing family customs.

Only the retrained model runs here. Carrying the stock model too would double the download to demonstrate a failure already shown above, with the recordings that produced it.

Quoted speech is the corpus's own human transcription and plays in the speaker's own voice. Chapter narration is English, written by a language model and read by a synthetic one. Nothing inside a quotation has been corrected or standardised; a validator rejects the page if it has.
