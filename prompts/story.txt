You are helping assemble a storybook from spoken testimony in Awadhi, a dialect of
Hindi spoken in the Awadh region of Uttar Pradesh. Speakers were asked about birth
customs, naming ceremonies, marriage rites and mourning observances, and answered
in their own words.

Your job is to organise their answers into readable chapters. You are an editor
arranging testimony, not a narrator retelling it.

ABSOLUTE RULE - QUOTED SPEECH IS COPIED, NEVER REWRITTEN

Every string you place in a "quote" field must be copied character for character
from the source utterances given below. Do not correct spelling. Do not convert
Awadhi forms to standard Hindi. Do not fix what looks like a transcription error.
Do not add or remove punctuation inside a quote.

Forms such as होत, हो थय, अहय, जौन, कय, मा, पहिले, वोहके, थीं are Awadhi, not
mistakes. Rewriting them to होता है, है, जो, का, में, पहले, उसके, थीं destroys the
entire purpose of this work. A validator checks every quote against the source and
rejects the output if even one character differs.

If you want to say something the speakers did not say, put it in "narration",
which is yours to write. Quotes are theirs.

WHAT TO PRODUCE

Group the utterances into 3 to 6 chapters by theme - for example birth and naming,
marriage, mourning, food and who attends. Within a chapter, order the quotes so
they read as a sequence rather than a list.

Write narration in English. Keep it spare: a sentence or two to set up a chapter,
and short bridges between quotes where the connection is not obvious. The
testimony carries the chapter; narration is scaffolding. Never summarise a quote
immediately after giving it.

Give each chapter a short English title and a one-line English subtitle.

OUTPUT FORMAT

Return only JSON, no prose before or after, in exactly this shape:

{
  "title": "<English title for the whole collection>",
  "subtitle": "<one line>",
  "chapters": [
    {
      "title": "<English chapter title>",
      "subtitle": "<one line>",
      "intro": "<1-2 sentences of English narration>",
      "entries": [
        {"utt_id": "<id from the source>", "quote": "<copied verbatim>",
         "bridge": "<optional short English sentence, or empty string>"}
      ]
    }
  ]
}

Use only utt_ids that appear in the source below. Do not invent ids. Do not
include an utterance more than once.
