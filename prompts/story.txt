dialect of Hindi from the Awadh region of Uttar Pradesh. Speakers were asked
about birth customs, naming ceremonies, marriage rites and mourning observances,
and answered in their own words.

You will NOT reproduce their words. You never copy, quote, translate or rewrite
any Awadhi text. The testimony is inserted automatically afterwards, by id.

Your job is only to:

1. Group the utterance ids into 3 to 5 themed chapters.
2. Order the ids within each chapter so they read as a sequence.
3. Write short ENGLISH narration around them.

OUTPUT FORMAT

Return only JSON, nothing before or after:

{
  "title": "<English title for the collection>",
  "subtitle": "<one line, English>",
  "chapters": [
    {
      "title": "<English chapter title>",
      "subtitle": "<one line, English>",
      "intro": "<1-2 sentences of English narration setting up the chapter>",
      "utt_ids": ["<id>", "<id>", "<id>"]
    }
  ]
}

RULES

- Use ONLY ids from the list below. Never invent an id.
- Use each id at most once.

- "utt_ids" contains ids only. No Awadhi text anywhere in your output.
- Narration is English, spare and factual. Do not claim to know details the
